- **Candidate groceries:** a two-portion candidate showed additional needs of 200 g pasta, 100 g tomatoes, 1 zucchini, 150 g spinach and 2 garlic. A later four-portion preview showed 300 g pasta, 400 g tomatoes, 2 zucchini, 300 g spinach and 4 garlic against that household's current stock and demand. Previewing did not add a batch or change live groceries or the saved shopping horizon.
- **Calendar scope and resumed context:** expanding to 28 days and adding breakfast/snack retained all four existing allocations and two coverages. Selecting dinner on October 20 previewed ingredients through that date, with an explicit notice that the saved shop-through date remained October 14; live groceries still had no upcoming uncooked batches. Reload retained 28 days, all four slots, the vegetable-dinner/25-minute constraint, skillet and rice cooker, and the six covered calendar rows.
- **Uncertain candidate stock:** after the partner marked pasta as “Some,” the October 20 candidate showed a 200 g pasta stock check, without inventing an exact shortage. Other known shortages remained unchanged. Live groceries still had no upcoming batches, and the saved horizon remained October 14.
- **Purchase detail and history:** a 500 g pasta purchase with purchased-on October 8, 2026, best-before October 8, 2027, source “Development pilot test” and lot `PILOT-AUDIT-1` persisted after reload. All historical date labels include the year. Spinach and garlic purchases without dates remained unknown; a 300 g spinach history entry still said “Purchase date not recorded” after cooking, while current stock was 0 g. Purchase history records amounts bought, not quantities remaining.
- **Prepared-food correction:** after cooking six portions with two in the freezer, marking the owner's Friday dinner eaten and saving four-star/make-again feedback, a correction changed actual production to seven, freezer portions to three and reopened that eaten allocation. Database hashes for pantry, ingredient usage, purchase lots and feedback were exactly unchanged across revisions 46 → 47. Corrections change prepared-food balances and consumption records; they do not refund ingredients or edit purchase history. Pantry corrections remain a separate explicit action.
- **Shared correction conflicts:** the partner received the seven-portion total. While the owner held an unsaved eight-portion correction and reason, the partner marked their dinner eaten. The owner's draft remained visible, saving was disabled and “Load current portions” was offered. Loading current values showed seven produced, one consumed and three frozen: six portions remained. No stale absolute correction overwrote the partner's update.
- **Rejected alternatives:** after saving a quick candidate and choosing “Not this one,” reload excluded it from current candidate focus and cards. Its earlier transcript entry remained available through an explicit “Reconsider” action, preserving the user's ability to deliberately revive the recipe.
- **Live rejection refresh:** rejecting the quick candidate in Local AI queued exactly one new job (household count 17 → 18). It completed in 32.059 s, returned no calendar operations or new recipe candidates, and suggested existing alternatives in its reply without recommending the rejected recipe. The unsent composer draft remained intact. Twenty seconds after completion there were still 18 jobs and none active, demonstrating that the response did not cause another refresh. Explicit “Reconsider” subsequently revived the recipe.
