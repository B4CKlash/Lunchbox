# Household pilot browser verification

Verified locally on 2026-10-08 using the actual application UI, local Supabase, and independent browser origins for owner and partner accounts. This is development evidence; a protected hosted preview and the real household shopping/cooking cycle remain separate gates.

## Completed flows

- Conversation and calendar: the authored fixture marks only the requesting person's Tuesday lunch. Vegetables produce an honestly labeled candidate; a quicker revision replaces focus without moving meals. Saving a favorite preserves the recipe snapshot.
- Batches: a six-portion pasta batch covers three two-person occasions. Applying its reviewed proposal counts ingredients once. Removing one person's allocation reduces planned yield, and marking that person's occasion as eating out leaves other allocations intact.
- Selected placements: a six-portion, three-occasion proposal can be reduced to four accepted placements. The saved batch has four portions, the unselected Friday lunch remains open, and Undo restores the original six-placement proposal. The same selection controls show the adjusted yield on mobile. Excluding both Friday placements, visiting Pantry, returning, and fully reloading preserves the four-of-six review choice in the owner’s cache; the partner’s independent review still has all six selected. A material household change marks the proposal stale and clears obsolete review choices.
- Purchases: buying pasta then cancelling its uncooked batch leaves the purchased pasta in inventory. Purchases are independent household events.
- Cooking and prepared food: a four-portion batch records actual yield and two frozen portions; allocating a prepared portion to a later date does not create another ingredient requirement. A five-star rating, make-again choice, and cooking notes persist after leaving and returning.
- Uncertain stock: marking tomatoes as “some” produces a stock check rather than an invented shortage. Confirming enough resolves the current requirement. The pantry displays “Some · amount unknown”; the measured-total editor starts blank. Entering the former historical amount explicitly (800 g) restores exact stock rather than silently retaining uncertainty.
- Shared identity: the partner's independent session saw the owner's Tuesday lunch change while its own stayed open (1/28 covered). The partner's own “my Tuesday lunch” then covered only the partner (2/28). Both sessions showed the same saved conversation and calendar.
- Conversation attribution: a new partner-authored message appears as “You” on that person's device and “Partner” on the owner's device. Earlier messages without an author remain visible as “Household member”; their authors are not guessed. The unfinished composer draft survived this check.
- Proposal recovery: dismissing a stale proposal removes it without changing the calendar; Undo restores it, including its original stale status.
- Resumption: changing to Local AI and typing an unsent message survives reload, including the selected mode. Composer metadata stays scoped to this browser, account, household and planning session; it does not change the revision of an active AI job.
- Unsaved settings: changing the visible week while a session-preferences draft is open preserves the draft and disables its stale Save button. Loading current settings explicitly updates the form; a clean form follows later calendar changes. Browsing keeps the original shopping horizon.
- Individual-preference conflicts: the owner kept an unsaved partner-preferences draft while the partner saved a different value from the other browser. The owner saw the new conflict warning, retained the exact draft, and could not save over the newer value. “Load current people” explicitly loaded the shared value and enabled the form again.
- Offline and cancellation: stopping the real Mac worker shows “Local AI is offline · request queued.” Cancel request reaches “Request cancelled” with a retry control. Manual inventory changes remain usable.
- Relevant interactions: selecting a different gap in Local AI mode queues an advisory refresh while preserving an unfinished message. A later gap cancels the earlier refresh. Submitting a typed request cancels the refresh and takes priority; further browsing does not supersede that manual request or continuously generate new ones. This was confirmed in the mobile UI and the local job history while the worker was stopped. With the pinned 27B worker running, selecting Sunday dinner returned an advisory context-refresh response, preserved the unfinished composer text and focused quick-pasta recipe, and left the four accepted allocations and grocery requirements unchanged. No repeated refresh was generated by that result.
- Cross-device priority: an owner-authored request remained queued when the partner selected a new meal gap from a stale assistant view. The server deferred the partner's refresh and the partner UI showed the owner's active request. The original request was then explicitly cancelled for test cleanup.
- Mobile: the conversation, calendar and food/shopping views retain focus at 390×844. No horizontal overflow was found. Reopening one person's meal offers one available portion rather than zero or the whole household. Stock checks remain qualitative. The final pinned-model conversation was reopened at 390×844: the unsent draft, four accepted batch allocations, focused Sunday dinner, and grocery preview persisted; document width was 375 px within the 390 px viewport.

## Real local AI boundary

A real browser request reached the authenticated Mac worker and returned a saved conversation and reviewable proposal. The initial 9B result correctly identified the owner but proposed Thursday October 8 for a Tuesday request. The review UI exposed this date error, and the proposal was not applied. This was treated as a failed interpretation and added to stricter date/actor evaluation; it is not evidence that the local model release gate passed.

The pinned 27B model subsequently completed an authenticated browser request, “Put this on Sunday dinner for both of us, one portion each.” Despite the focused slot being Sunday lunch, it placed the exact existing recipe on Sunday October 11 dinner for both people, showed an applied receipt with Undo, and synchronized both allocations to the partner browser. Undo reopened both meals. The model’s advisory wording is followed by the application’s own durable-save confirmation. Evidence: `.local/pilot-local-ai-direct-placement.png`.

The same real conversation retrieved the unchanged favorite, generated a new tomato/spinach/zucchini pasta, and revised it from 25 to 18 minutes through the Less effort control. It then proposed one six-portion batch prepared Friday October 9 for Friday dinner, Saturday lunch, and Sunday lunch for both people. Accepting only Friday and Saturday saved one four-portion batch, kept Sunday open, and showed shortages of 400 g dry pasta, 300 g spinach, and 4 each garlic; tomatoes, zucchini, and oil were covered by matching stock. Base recipe quantities were inspected in the UI. Generated recipe details still need review: this recipe's steps mention salted water without listing a salt amount.

Observed database enqueue-to-completion times for these live requests were 25.5 s (direct placement), 11.6 s (favorite), 28.5 s (new vegetables), 30.1 s (quicker revision), and 62.0 s (broad batch). These include queue and completion transport and use a populated shared conversation, so they are not the same timing boundary or inputs as the smaller evaluation fixtures. Separate queue/run time and correction counts are not persisted. Sanitized local evidence is in `.local/pinned-worker-browser-latencies.json`.

The recipe library separately returned three validated AI recipes from the pinned worker (61.151 s generation, one attempt, three accepted). Pasted-text extraction also used that worker (9.880 s generation). It preserved a cup measure as unknown, blocked saving until missing servings, time, and supported measurement were entered, and durably saved the reviewed recipe with imported provenance. The combined “Serves 2. Total time…” source line remained descriptive text and required those two fields to be confirmed manually. Together with the planning browser flows and the documented legacy-chat retry, all four worker job kinds reached validated completion; the original chat failure remains recorded.

Current quality reports and the final model choice belong in [evaluations](evaluations/) and [the delivery tracker](HOUSEHOLD_PILOT_DELIVERY.md). No inferred cooking or purchasing occurred merely because a date passed or a model claimed completion.
