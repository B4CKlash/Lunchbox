## Delivered behavior

The pantry and planning assistant share a reviewed natural-entry draft. Original wording, canonical ingredient identity, amount, unit or container kind, certainty, and **Set current total** versus **Add stock** stay visible before saving. Apples use `each`; grams remain grams. Can, bag, jar, box, and bottle counts are separate observations with unknown contents. Exact container counts must be whole numbers; opened or partial containers retain qualitative stock and their wording. Ambiguous ingredient forms require review rather than a guessed canonical identity.

Adding stock to an uncertain balance preserves that uncertainty. The stock-truth regression keeps historical 900 g, subsequently marked Some, plus an imported 100 g from becoming an invented exact 1,000 g. The model receives one current ingredient/unit projection. Container purchases retain their exact historical counts and retry identifiers without changing measured balances.

Shopping and cooking share the same sufficiency calculation. When measured stock is insufficient and packages may cover the remainder, the app shows known availability and an unverified remainder in a stock check instead of also calling that remainder a definite purchase. Changes to relevant demand, measured stock, or package observations reopen the check. Cooking requires current confirmations where needed, preserves ordinary measured deductions when sufficient, and makes affected remaining container counts unknown instead of inventing package consumption or remaining weight. Undo restores stock observations, checks, and purchase history together.

Planning, suggestions, and recipe chat use the same bounded recommendation context: selected people, current household and relevant personal preferences, equipment, session constraints, attributed feedback, exact favorites, canonical stock, and package observations. Direct recipe requests carry the typed context; authenticated workers rebuild it from the saved household and authenticated actor. Current-message corrections can affect the answer before persistence without becoming an independent write. Fixture and demo recommendations also respect food and equipment constraints and explain when no sample recipe fits. Imported recipe extraction retains its original recipe quantities and content.

Semantic context changes cancel or reject obsolete recommendations and label earlier recipe blocks. Transcript growth, receipts, composer edits, revision bookkeeping, and suggestion history do not restart the feed. The semantic fingerprint is a comparison token; revision and completed-job checks still authorize writes.

Explicit facts save through the existing command boundary. Personal edits and new feedback belong to the authenticated person; another person cannot take over an existing feedback ID, including legacy household feedback. Household preferences and kitchen equipment remain shared. A member resuming another member's exact completed job preserves the original speaker and provenance. Unknown or unresolved actors do not get personal editing controls through a first-person fallback.

Recipe chat keeps the completed job identity through atomic transcript/profile acceptance. It flushes queued household edits before requesting or retrying a reply. A cached reply is reused only for the same request, semantic context, and unchanged base revision, and only if its assistant message is not already saved. Remote persistence failures retain the original command for the household notice's **Retry saving** action; local failures retain the reply and draft. The Remembered receipt appears only after persistence succeeds. Undo preserves both transcripts and the current draft, and history cannot recreate an undone fact.
